this tiny chai stall sells over three hundred cups a day
but how much does the owner actually take home
setting up costs around fifty thousand rupees a cart a stove and some utensils
each cup sells for fifteen rupees so daily sales reach four thousand five hundred rupees
milk tea leaves sugar gas and cups eat up about two thousand seven hundred rupees
that leaves roughly one thousand eight hundred rupees a day or over fifty thousand rupees a month
not bad for a cart on the corner
